FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is CyberCodix CRM?

CyberCodix CRM is a customer communication platform combining WhatsApp Business API, lead management, team inbox, campaigns, automation, AI and integrations.

Can multiple agents use one WhatsApp number?

Yes. CyberCodix is designed around a shared team inbox so authorized agents can manage conversations from one workspace.

Can I connect Meta Lead Ads?

Yes. Connect eligible Facebook and Instagram lead sources and route new leads into your CRM and automated WhatsApp workflows.

Can CyberCodix automate follow-ups?

Yes. Build workflows using triggers, conditions, delays, messages, assignments and notifications.

Does CyberCodix support templates?

Yes. Use approved WhatsApp templates for business-initiated messaging outside the customer-service window.

Can developers use an API?

Yes. CyberCodix can expose API and webhook functionality for custom applications and integrations.
